What is actually being modeled

Peptide mass is the sum of average residue masses plus one water (18.015 Da) for the two termini. Every new peptide bond is a condensation: the backbone loses H from the amino group and OH from the carboxyl, and those leave as water.

Charge uses fixed textbook pKa values: N-terminus 9.69, C-terminus 2.34, Asp 3.9, Glu 4.3, His 6.04, Cys 8.33, Tyr 10.07, Lys 10.54, Arg 12.48. Real peptides shift these depending on neighbors. The pI is the pH where the summed charge crosses zero.

GRAVY is the mean Kyte–Doolittle score. The aliphatic index follows Ikai: mole percent of Ala + 2.9 Val + 3.9 (Ile + Leu). Absorbance at 280 nm counts Trp at 5500 and Tyr at 1490 M⁻¹ cm⁻¹, assuming reduced cysteines. Each disulfide would add about 125.

Secondary structure is Chou and Fasman, 1978. Helix needs four of six residues with Pα above 1, then extension, and the segment must beat the sheet score. Turns use the positional-frequency product. It was designed for whole proteins and is often wrong on short peptides. It will not tell you a three-dimensional fold.
